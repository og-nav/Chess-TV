# Sound auditions

## Compare six sets

Open `compare.html` for individual Move/Capture/Check buttons, each set's sequence,
six repeated moves, and a comparison of all six move sounds. It embeds the audio
and needs no network for playback. Rebuild with `python3 scripts/make-sound-gallery.py`
(requires ffmpeg). This generator never writes app resources.

The options are recorded wood (current reference), muted wood (lower pitch and
filtered high frequencies), the earlier soft felt candidate, and the Lichess
Piano, NES, and SFX sets. All preview clips have been level-matched. The Lichess
originals, pinned source URLs, hashes, license and upstream credits are preserved
under `sources/lichess/`; Enigmahack's three sets are explicitly AGPLv3+ in the
upstream copying notice. All six are now selectable in the app's Settings; the
shipping set clips match the gallery exactly. Recorded Wood remains the default.

## Current: recorded wood (21 September 2026)

The default Recorded Wood move/capture/check use CC0 recordings of real wooden chess pieces.
Check is a quieter double tap, with no electronic chime. The game-over sound is unchanged.
Listen to `recorded-wood-preview.wav`: move at 0.35 s, capture at 1.5 s, check at
2.7 s, followed by six ordinary moves starting at 4.0 s. Individual clips and
playback controls are in `recorded-wood.html`.
See [source and processing details](../../assets/audio/wooden-chess/README.md).

## Earlier synthesized candidates

Original synthesized candidates. The owner selected **A — Wood, move only** on 20 September 2026. Generated by
`scripts/make-sound-auditions.py` using Python's standard library; no recordings or third-party samples.

| Set | Direction | Check | Game over |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A — Wood | Short, textured, woody impact | A lighter double tap | Three descending wooden taps |
| B — Felt | Softer, damped piece landing | A tap with a faint high mallet tone | A gentle descending three-note figure |
| C — Digital | Short tonal plucks | A brighter rising pair | A descending three-note figure |

Each `*-audition.wav` plays move at 0.35s, capture at 1.65s, check at 3.0s, and game over at 4.55s.
Each `*-move-loop.wav` repeats the ordinary move six times to judge whether it would become tiring.
`move-comparison-A-B-C.wav` plays two moves from each set, A then B then C.

Individual events are in the corresponding set directory. All are 44.1 kHz, 16-bit mono PCM WAV.
The generator matches short-event energy with a -5.5 dBFS peak ceiling and fades file edges to silence.
`manifest.json` records exact durations and peaks. The candidate files have been checked for valid
PCM, clipping and reproducible output; subjective listening and selection are still the owner's call.

The previous production move matched `A-wood/move.wav` byte for byte. These earlier
auditions remain available for comparison; they are no longer the production set.
